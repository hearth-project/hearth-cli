# Code of Conduct

Hearth follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in issues, pull requests, reviews and every other project space, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect different opinions and experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information (such as an address, email or credentials) without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers: open a [private report](https://github.com/hearth-project/hearth-cli/security/advisories/new) whose title starts with "Code of conduct". Only maintainers can see it. Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor, following the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant.
