# Getting help

- **Docs first.** [Quick start](docs/en/quickstart.md), [Usage](docs/en/usage.md), [Server](docs/en/server.md) and [Troubleshooting](docs/en/troubleshooting.md), in English and [Chinese](docs/zh/).
- **Ask the machine.** `hearth status` shows login, background sync, storage and what is left to do; `hearth doctor` checks the setup. Your agent can do the same: say *"use hearth"*.
- **Bugs and feature requests:** open an [issue](https://github.com/hearth-project/hearth-cli/issues/new/choose). Include `hearth --version`, the OS and architecture, and the output of `hearth status`. Remove email addresses, host names and tokens you do not want to share.
- **Planned work:** the [development roadmap](https://github.com/hearth-project/hearth-cli/issues/1).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** report them privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Never in a public issue.
